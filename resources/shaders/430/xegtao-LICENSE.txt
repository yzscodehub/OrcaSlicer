GLSL port based on GameTechDev/XeGTAO commit a5b1686c7ea37788eeb3576b5be47f7c03db532c.
Changes: FP32 OpenGL depth/normal coordinates, orthographic reconstruction, padded depth tiles,
explicit background and image bounds, R8UI edges, normalized final output, fixed temporal index.
Bent normals are not included. Original formulas, constants and denoiser are retained.
