CS adaptation: FP32 OpenGL depth/normal coordinates, orthographic reconstruction,
padded depth tiles, explicit background and image bounds, R8UI edges,
normalized final output, fixed temporal index, and Low unoccluded-slice
normalization with LOD0 sampling. Bent normals are not included.

FS edge adaptation: normalized R8 visibility, RGBA8 directional edges,
and fragment-stage edge-guided filtering without CS visibility encoding.

Experimental adaptation: ao_denoise_visibility_compact_experimental.fs, compact edge-guided denoising kernel.
Source: https://github.com/GameTechDev/XeGTAO/blob/a5b1686c7ea37788eeb3576b5be47f7c03db532c/Source/Rendering/Shaders/XeGTAO.hlsli
Local upstream revision: a5b1686c7ea37788eeb3576b5be47f7c03db532c
Changes: GLSL 140, RGBA8 edge channels in left/right/down/up order, no deliberate
edge leakage, explicit screen bounds, normalized R8 visibility, no bent normals,
no texture Gather batching or upstream visibility term scaling.
